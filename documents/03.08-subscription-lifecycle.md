# Subscription lifecycle

PayGate owns your subscriptions: it tracks the billing period, reminds the customer by email, and takes payment through **any payment method your app has enabled** (Stripe, PayOS, bank transfer, …). There is no automatic card charge — a subscription renews when the customer pays again.

## End-to-end flow

1. **Create a customer** once per person you bill — [`POST /api/v1/customers`](03.06-customers.html). Keep the `cus_…` id.
2. **Create a plan with its prices** once — [`POST /api/v1/plans`](03.07-plans-and-prices.html). Keep the `price_…` ids (e.g. monthly and yearly).
3. **Start a checkout** with `mode: "subscription"`, a `price_id` and the `customer_id` — [`POST /api/v1/checkout-sessions`](03.01-checkout-sessions.html).
4. **Activation**
   - Price without a trial, or `skip_trial: true`, or a trial already used: the end user pays on `checkout_url`. On payment the subscription becomes `active` with `current_period_end` = now + the price's interval.
   - Price with a **free trial**: the subscription is activated during the checkout call itself (`activated: true`) and the response `checkout_url` is already your `success_url`. `in_trial` is `true` and `current_period_end` (= `trial_ends_at`) is the end of the trial.
   - Price with a **paid trial**: the end user pays the trial amount; the first period lasts for the trial's interval.
5. **Confirm** via the [`subscription.updated` webhook](04-webhooks.html) or [`GET /api/v1/subscriptions/{external_ref}`](03.02-subscriptions.html) — never from the redirect alone.
6. **Reminders** go to the customer before the period ends (below).
7. **Renewal**: the customer opens the link in the email, pays, and you receive `subscription.renewed` and `subscription.updated`.

## Access, expiry and the grace window

- Access ends at `current_period_end`. As soon as that moment passes, `GET /api/v1/subscriptions/{external_ref}` reports `status: "expired"` — treat that as "no access".
- The **renewal grace window is 7 days**: for 7 days after `current_period_end` the renewal link still works so the customer can pay again. This is only a window to *pay*; it does **not** extend access.
- A payment made **while the subscription is still valid** extends it from the current `current_period_end` (no time is lost). A payment made **after it expired** starts a new period from the time of payment.
- Renewals are always charged at the price's regular amount, never at the trial amount.
- A trial ending behaves exactly like an expiry: nothing is charged automatically; the customer gets the same reminders and renews by paying.

## Reminder emails and the renewal page

For each billing period PayGate emails the customer:

| When | Email |
| --- | --- |
| 3 days before `current_period_end` | Renewal reminder |
| 1 day before `current_period_end` | Renewal reminder |
| After `current_period_end` (within the grace window) | One "expired" notice |

Each email goes **To** the customer's `email`, with the customer's `cc_emails` as Cc and `bcc_emails` as Bcc, written in the customer's `locale` (`en` or `vi`). A stage that no longer applies is skipped (for example, a period shorter than 3 days gets no 3-day reminder). Each stage is sent at most once per period. Reminders need a customer attached to the subscription (see [`PUT .../customer`](03.02-subscriptions.html)).

Every email links to `https://<gateway>/renew/{token}`, a hosted page that lists **every payment method your app has enabled** for the customer to pay with. PayGate hosts the whole renewal; confirmation reaches you through webhooks, not a redirect to your `success_url`. The email also includes an opt-out link; a customer who uses it stops receiving reminders for that subscription. You are notified of each reminder with the `subscription.renewal_reminder_sent` webhook (it never contains the link).

Webhooks you receive during the lifecycle: `subscription.updated` (every change), `subscription.renewed` (payment applied to an existing subscription, including when a trial converts), `subscription.renewal_reminder_sent` — see [04 — Webhooks](04-webhooks.html).

## Switching monthly to yearly

Call [`PUT /api/v1/subscriptions/{external_ref}/plan`](03.02-subscriptions.html) with the yearly `price_id` (HMAC apps only):

- The current period is untouched and nothing is refunded or prorated.
- From the **next renewal**, the reminders, the renewal link and the amount use the new price, and the subscription's `plan_ref`, `plan_id` and `price_id` already report the new values.
- Any price on sale works — another price of the same plan, or a price of a different plan.

## One trial per end user and plan

A trial is granted at most once per end user and plan, **across all of the plan's prices**. If someone used the trial on Pro monthly, a later checkout on Pro yearly gets no trial. `skip_trial: true` skips it even when it would be available.

## Quick reference

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is anything charged automatically? | No — the customer pays via the renewal link or a new checkout |
| When does access stop? | At `current_period_end` |
| How long can the customer still renew after that? | 7 days (grace window), starting a new period from the payment time |
| Payment before the end? | Extends from `current_period_end` |
| Which language are emails in? | The customer's `locale` |
